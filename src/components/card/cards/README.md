# OpenDecks playing cards

This directory contains the 52 standard playing-card fronts and the two card backs from [OpenDecks](https://github.com/AustinGabriel/OpenDecks-Public-Domain-and-CC0-Playing-Cards).

The SVG assets are released under the [CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They may be used and modified without attribution. The two joker files from the source deck are intentionally not included.

The “OpenDecks” name is a trademark of its owner. The SVG assets themselves remain CC0.

## File names

Card fronts use the compact `<suit><rank>.svg` convention:

- suits: `c` (clubs), `d` (diamonds), `h` (hearts), `s` (spades);
- ranks: `a`, `2` through `10`, `j`, `q`, `k`.

For example, `s2.svg` is the two of spades and `dk.svg` is the king of diamonds. Card backs are named `back-blue.svg` and `back-red.svg`.
